Training Available

I provide safety-focused emergency response training designed to be clear, practical, and easy to apply in real workplace and community settings.

First Aid

Basic first aid response for common injuries, sudden illness, bleeding, burns, shock, and other emergency situations.

CPR

Hands-on CPR training focused on recognizing cardiac emergencies and responding quickly and confidently.

AED

Training on how to safely and effectively use an automated external defibrillator during a cardiac emergency.

Bloodborne Pathogens

Workplace-focused training on exposure risks, prevention practices, cleanup awareness, and protective measures.

Anaphylaxis

Training on recognizing severe allergic reactions and understanding emergency response steps, including epinephrine auto-injector awareness.

Opioid Overdose

Training on recognizing possible opioid overdose symptoms and understanding emergency response actions, including naloxone awareness.

Who This Is For

Workplaces

Small Businesses

Convenient training options for teams that need practical emergency response preparation.

Community Groups

Useful for coaches, parents, volunteers, youth programs, and local organizations.
