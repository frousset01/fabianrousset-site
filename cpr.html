Training Available

I provide safety-focused emergency response training designed to be clear, practical, and easy to apply in workplaces and communities.

First Aid

Basic first aid response for common injuries, sudden illness, bleeding, burns, shock, and other emergencies.

CPR

Hands-on CPR training focused on recognizing cardiac emergencies and responding quickly and confidently.

AED

Training on how to safely and effectively use an automated external defibrillator during a cardiac emergency.

Bloodborne Pathogens

Workplace-focused training on exposure risks, prevention practices, cleanup awareness, and protective measures.

Anaphylaxis

Training on recognizing severe allergic reactions and understanding emergency response steps including epinephrine awareness.

Opioid Overdose

Training on recognizing opioid overdose symptoms and understanding emergency response actions including naloxone awareness.
